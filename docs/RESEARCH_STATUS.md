## Terminology

- **TECHNICAL FULL:** its audit program ran and integrity checks passed; says nothing by itself about qualified scientific sampling.
- **SMOKE:** synthetic or startup testing; no genuine benchmark accuracy claim.
- **DEVELOPMENT:** real training/evaluation using provisional permitted data; not a certified FULL result.
- **CERTIFIED/FORMAL FULL:** only after the blueprint's scientific gates and relevant experiment controls are satisfied.

No score should be transferred between cohorts or described as comparable without a documented common protocol, identities and evaluation masks.
